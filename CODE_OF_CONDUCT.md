# Code of Conduct

OpenSpec Workbench contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, inclusive,
and harassment-free community.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical feedback, respect for
different experience levels and perspectives, clear attribution, and care with
security or private information. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private data, and
sustained disruption of project collaboration.

Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and restrict
participation when behavior threatens people or the project. Enforcement will
be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and applied without
retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately through a verified contact method on the
[repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/mykola-melnik). Do not
put sensitive reports in a public issue. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, subject to legal or immediate-safety obligations.
